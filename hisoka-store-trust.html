POLICY

Warranty & refund policy

Covered under warranty

  • Account reclaimed by the original owner within the warranty period
  • Account details (rank, heroes, skins) don't match what was listed
  • Login credentials stop working through no fault of the buyer

Not covered

  • Bans from cheating, boosting, or rule violations after handover
  • Buyer changing the bound email/phone and losing access as a result
  • Sharing login credentials with a third party after purchase

Payment methods

  • UPI (₹)
  • Binance ($)

How a warranty claim works

1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and what went wrong.
2. We verify the issue against the warranty terms above.
3. If covered, you get a replacement account or a refund — your choice.

This is a plain-language summary, not a legal document — it's worth having a lawyer turn this into formal terms before the store goes live, especially around refund timelines and payment disputes.
